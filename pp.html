Privacy Policy

How Velora Maps handles information when you explore, request online data, or use Velora Pro.

Effective date: September 29, 2026

1. Information used by the app

Velora Maps uses location permission, if you grant it, to show your position and provide route guidance. Map downloads, saved places, preferences, and other app data can be stored on your device. You can change location permission in iPhone Settings and remove local app data by deleting the app.

2. Online services

Downloading maps, retrieving online content, and requesting other connected features send network requests. Those requests can include technical information such as your IP address, device or app version, and the area or content you requested. Online services use that information to return the requested result and maintain the service. Offline maps remain available without a network connection after they are downloaded.

3. Subscriptions

Apple processes purchases and billing for Velora Pro. We use subscription services, including Adapty and SubsSub, to present plans and determine whether Pro features are available. These services may process purchase status, subscription identifiers, and technical identifiers needed to manage access. We do not receive your full payment card details from Apple.

4. Velora assistant

On supported devices, Velora uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models. Your request is processed on your device for this feature. If a future version adds an online assistant, this policy will be updated to explain that processing before it is introduced.

5. Diagnostics and support

The app and its service providers may process diagnostic information, such as app performance and error details, to keep Velora Maps working. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information unless it is needed for support.

6. Third-party data and links

Map content is based in part on OpenStreetMap and other data sources. Links to Apple, OpenStreetMap, and other services are governed by their own privacy policies. Their handling of data can differ from ours.

7. Your choices

  • Change or revoke location permission in iPhone Settings.
  • Manage or cancel a Velora Pro subscription in your Apple Account subscriptions.
  • Contact us to ask about information you provided directly to support.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when Velora Maps changes. The effective date above will show when this page was last updated. For privacy questions, email loopenders@gmail.com.
